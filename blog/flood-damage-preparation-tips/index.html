Flood Damage Preparation Tips in Las Cruces, NM

Home / Blog / Flood Prevention for Homeowners in Las Cruces, NM

The weather is out of your hands, but you can act early to shield a property in Las Cruces, NM against flood damage. Being prepared significantly softens the blow of a flood and may save you thousands on restoration. This guide from Las Cruces Restoration covers the most effective preparation strategies for Las Cruces homeowners.

Know Your Flood Risk

Understanding your property's flood risk is the first step in preparation. Check FEMA flood maps to determine whether your property is in a designated flood zone. Even if you're not in a high-risk zone, consider that most flood claims come from outside designated flood zones. Look at how your land slopes, how it drains, and how close it sits to any waterway that might overflow in heavy rain.

Ways to Protect the Property

On the physical side, keep a sump pump with a backup battery installed and maintained, store valuables and key documents higher than ground level, grade the lot to fall away from the foundation, lift electrical panels and HVAC units above where floodwater could reach, seal any foundation cracks and add a waterproof coating, and clean gutters and downspouts while running the discharge out to 6 feet or farther from the foundation.

Getting Your Insurance in Order

Standard homeowner's insurance does not cover flood damage from rising water. Consider purchasing flood insurance through the NFIP or a private carrier, and since there is typically a 30-day waiting period before coverage begins, don't wait for a forecast to act. Document your property and belongings with photos and video for insurance purposes. Keep insurance documents accessible and know your coverage limits and deductible.

Emergency Response Plan

Planning has to happen before floodwater is on the way. Pack a go-bag and learn the routes you would take to evacuate.
